# Sibeliuksen puisto #1324

Sisäänrakennettu ImageGen, yksi kokonaan uusi kuva. Kolme tilauksen Helsinki-mallia tyylireferensseinä. Läpinäkyvä tausta pyydetty.

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE entirely new map-landmark miniature of Sibelius Park in Töölö, Helsinki: a compact rocky seaside park vignette WITHOUT the Sibelius Monument.

The three attached images are STYLE REFERENCES ONLY: 1 Kaisaniemi Park (main park/watercolor style reference), 2 Helsinki Cathedral, 3 Uspenski Cathedral. Match their elevated three-quarter map view, softly modeled daylight from upper left, fine warm grey-brown hand-drawn outlines and naturally shaded watercolor washes. Match the normal gentle watercolor tonal range and muted natural colors; do not intensify contrast, brighten, bleach, or add glossy color. Do not reproduce the reference buildings, glass pavilion, background or sky.

Show a centered compact low park island: smooth rounded glacial granite bedrock with gently flowing contours and restrained natural fissures, a small cluster of identifiable white-barked birches with irregular leafy crowns, a few Finnish Scots pines with naturally branching trunks and airy evergreen crowns, modest patches of lawn, and a pale gently curving park path. Include one simple unobtrusive wooden park bench beside the path if it supports readability. A NARROW connected strip of calm blue-grey seawater runs along just one edge of the rocky shoreline. The sea strip is part of the vignette, not a surrounding ocean, full background or horizon. Balance rock, trees, path and grass as one connected compact miniature; avoid a broad empty lawn. This is a stylized representative park vignette, not a surveyed site plan. Natural irregular rock and foliage detail without stamped or repeating texture. Trees, rock and bench rest coherently on the ground. Tree species must read clearly even at small map size.

Composition and output: square 1024 x 1024, transparent alpha outside the complete vignette, generous clear margin on all four sides, entire tree tops and shoreline visible, soft organic silhouette, no hard rectangular tile or tall extruded display slab. Center the actual subject. Preserve hand-illustrated colored-and-shaded character of the Helsinki miniatures.

Absolutely exclude the Sibelius Monument, abstract pipe sculptures, organ-like tubes, bundles of upright metal pipes, any face or portrait relief, musical notes or music stand, statues, buildings, people, vehicles, boats, text, labels, logos and watermarks. No frame, paper-colored backdrop, checkerboard, sky, horizon, distant city or scenery extending to the canvas edges. The background must be genuinely transparent even though the supplied reference previews have pale colored backgrounds. Make this a completely new illustration, not an edit of any reference.
