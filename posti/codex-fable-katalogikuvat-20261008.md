Poisjätetyt kohteet (ei toimitusta eikä uusintagenerointia):

- `yokartta`: Nighttime coastlines form overly continuous bright light chains and do not meet the required natural camera-photograph appearance. Original candidate preserved; no additional generation under the one-call cap.
- `maapallon-vuosi`: The northern polar ice and daylight are visible, but the requested clear tilt of the North Pole toward the Sun is not demonstrated unambiguously. Original candidate preserved; no additional generation under the one-call cap.
- `tavli`: Board has seven triangles in each of four quadrants (28 total) rather than a valid24-point backgammon board; piece counts also disagree. Original preserved, no replacement generation under the one-call cap.
