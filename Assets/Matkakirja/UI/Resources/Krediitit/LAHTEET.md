  paketti https://developers.google.com/static/maps/documentation/images/Google_Maps_Attribution_Assets.zip
  (kansio GoogleMaps_Logo_WithDarkOutline). Ladattu 6.10.2026 omistajan luvalla (Natiivi-UI).
